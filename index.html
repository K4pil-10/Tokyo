About Tokyo Revengers:

Season-1 — Tokyo Revengers

Season 1 introduces Takemichi Hanagaki, a young man who feels unhappy with his life. One day, he learns that his old girlfriend Hinata Tachibana and her younger brother Naoto have been killed by the Tokyo Manji Gang (Toman). Soon after, Takemichi is pushed in front of a train and suddenly travels 12 years into the past. Takemichi tells young Naoto about Hinata's future death. When he returns to the present, he discovers that Naoto is now a police officer and Hinata is still alive. Takemichi learns that he can travel between the past and future by shaking Naoto's hand. He meets important Toman members such as Mikey, Draken, Baji, and Mitsuya. Takemichi becomes involved in gang conflicts and tries to stop Toman from becoming a dangerous criminal group. He also discovers that Kisaki is secretly controlling many events. Takemichi faces many difficult fights but refuses to give up. He tries to save his friends and change the future. The season ends with a tragic battle involving Baji, making Takemichi even more determined to protect everyone.

Season-2 — Christmas Showdown

Season 2 begins when Takemichi returns to the future and discovers that things have become worse. Hinata is dead again, and Toman has become a dangerous criminal organization. Takemichi also learns that Mikey has changed and many of his friends are in danger. He travels back to the past and learns about the Black Dragon gang. Its leader, Taiju Shiba, is extremely strong and violent. Takemichi meets Taiju's younger brother Hakkai and sister Yuzuha. Hakkai wants to leave Toman because he is afraid of Taiju and wants to protect Yuzuha. During the Christmas Showdown, Takemichi and his friends fight against Taiju and the Black Dragons. Takemichi is badly beaten but continues fighting because he wants to protect Hakkai and his friends. Mitsuya, Chifuyu, and other Toman members also help during the battle. Later, Mikey and Draken arrive and help defeat the Black Dragons. After the battle, Takemichi returns to the future and finds that Hinata is alive. However, he learns that Mikey has disappeared, creating a new problem for him.

Season-3 — Tenjiku Arc

Season 3 focuses on the conflict between Toman and Tenjiku, a powerful gang led by Izana Kurokawa. Takemichi returns to the past and discovers that Tenjiku is attacking Toman members. He also learns that Kisaki is working with Tenjiku and is still trying to control Toman. Takemichi learns more about Izana's painful past and his connection with Shinichiro Sano, Mikey's older brother. Izana becomes jealous of Mikey and develops a strong hatred toward him. The conflict becomes more serious when Tenjiku attacks Toman. Many characters are injured, and Takemichi realizes that he must fight to protect his friends. He continues moving forward even when he is badly hurt. The final battle between Toman and Tenjiku is intense. Takemichi fights Kakucho, while Mikey faces Izana. Toman eventually wins the battle, but Izana dies. After the fight, Kisaki tries to escape. Takemichi follows him, and Kisaki dies in an accident. Takemichi believes he has finally changed the future, but Mikey creates another unexpected problem, leading to the next part of the story.

Gang of 東京リベンジャーズ

Tokyo Manji Gang(Toman)

The Tokyo Manji Gang, also known as Toman, is one of the most important gangs in Tokyo Revengers. It was founded by Mikey, Draken, Baji, Mitsuya, Pah-chin, and Kazutora. In the beginning, Toman was created mainly because the members wanted to protect each other and enjoy their friendship. Mikey became the leader, while Draken became the vice-leader. Toman has many strong fighters, including Baji, Mitsuya, Chifuyu, and later Takemichi. As the story continues, Toman becomes involved in many battles against other gangs. Unfortunately, after several changes, the gang becomes darker and more dangerous. Takemichi tries to change its future and protect his friends.

Black Dragon

The Black Dragon is an old and powerful gang in Tokyo Revengers. It was originally founded by Shinichiro Sano, Mikey's older brother. Shinichiro was respected because of his kindness and leadership. Later, the gang became much more violent under different leaders. During Season 2, Taiju Shiba becomes the leader of the Black Dragon. He is extremely strong and rules through fear. His younger brother Hakkai and sister Yuzuha suffer because of his violent behavior. Takemichi and his friends eventually fight against Taiju and the Black Dragon during the Christmas Showdown. The battle changes the lives of several characters and helps Takemichi create a better future.

Tenjiku Gang

Tenjiku is a powerful gang introduced mainly in Season 3. It is led by Izana Kurokawa, who is one of the strongest characters in the series. Kisaki Tetta also becomes an important member of Tenjiku and uses his intelligence to manipulate events. Tenjiku gathers many strong fighters, including Kakucho and other former gang members. Their goal is to defeat Toman and take control. The conflict between Toman and Tenjiku becomes known as the Tenjiku Arc. The final battle is very serious and causes many painful losses. In the end, Toman defeats Tenjiku, but the battle has a major effect on Mikey, Takemichi, and their friends.

Bonten Gang

Bonten is a powerful criminal organization in Tokyo Revengers. It appears in one of the future timelines created after Takemichi fails to change everything. Mikey becomes the leader of Bonten and turns into a much darker person. The organization is involved in many dangerous and illegal activities. Some important members of Bonten are Sanzu Haruchiyo, Kakucho, Ran Haitani, Rindou Haitani, Takeomi Akashi, and Kanji Mochizuki. Sanzu is one of Mikey's most loyal members and follows his orders. The Haitani brothers, Ran and Rindou, are also strong and important members. Bonten shows how much Mikey has changed from the kind leader of Toman. When Takemichi discovers Bonten, he realizes that changing the future is not enough. He also needs to understand why Mikey became so dark and find a way to save him. Bonten represents one of the darkest futures in the story.
